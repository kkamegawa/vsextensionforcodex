The build and workflow scripts write result manifests, sanitized per-attempt TRX/log evidence, skip reasons, retry outcomes, and artifact hashes. A missing result is `not-run`; it is not equivalent to a pass. Public CI never runs the External scenarios or uploads raw authenticated traffic.

PR #170 counts and hashes remain historical evidence. They do not satisfy the release-candidate matrix. The six historical Windows skips are not attributed to a cause unless their original test results identify one.
